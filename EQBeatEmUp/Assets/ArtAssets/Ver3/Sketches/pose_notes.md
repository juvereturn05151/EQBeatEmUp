# Hands-in-pockets construction guide

The first AI master candidate is saved here for comparison, not used as an animation frame. It looked too side-on to verify the far arm. The selected master in `Keyframe` is the revised, more readable torso view.

For a rough guide in Krita, draw simple shapes over the selected master on a separate layer: head oval, rib-cage wedge, pelvis block and a center-of-balance line. Mark both knees, ankles and shoes. Then draw each arm as shoulder → elbow → wrist-to-pocket. The hands are occluded by the pants, so don't sketch exterior fingers. Pocket-entry anchors should move with the pelvis, not the breathing chest.

At the 1024 working size, the lowest master sole is near y=948; the shared working pivot is `(512,948)`. The shoes are staggered in depth, so each has its own contact contour. Do not force both entire soles onto the same horizontal line. Keep each shoe's individual contact stable across poses.

Use a comfortable stance with the center of mass inside the two-foot support area. Keep elbows slightly away from the waist and the far forearm anatomically connected even if it is partly occluded. Avoid a crouch, giant chest expansion, shoulders pulled high, or an exaggerated backward lean.

Suggested control layers: `pose_neutral`, `pocket_anchors`, `foot_anchors`, `lineart_master`, `face_protect`. Keep them hidden for output. Use the same guide in every pose with only tiny shoulder/chest changes.
