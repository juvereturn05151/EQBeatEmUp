# Ver4 face and pocket-pose construction

Draw these guides on separate Krita layers above the master at low opacity; hide them for export.

## Face construction

Start with the friend's rounded skull, close buzz cut, forehead/nose profile and relative nose-mouth-chin spacing. Simplify rather than replace these identity anchors. Use a clearer angle at the jaw and a restrained cheek-plane mark. Keep the jaw strong without stretching the chin or enlarging the skull.

Use a bold upper eyelid, small dark pupil and compact eye-white wedge. A low, mostly straight brow establishes toughness. Keep gaze and eyelid shape calm rather than furiously pinched. A short firm mouth line and one lower-lip accent are enough. Use a flat skin fill, one connected shadow under jaw/neck, and very few internal face marks. Avoid modelling every plane with gradients or tiny wrinkles.

The intended influence is classic delinquent manga attitude, including the requested Rokudenashi Blues mood, expressed through original graphic choices. No existing character's hair, scar, costume or face is a design template. Reference A remains the identity source; Reference B supplies only the rough ink/print vocabulary.

## Pocket-pose construction

Mark head, rib cage, pelvis, shoulders, elbows, wrist-to-pocket entries, knees and two individual shoe contacts. Draw both arms continuously from sleeve to elbow to pocket. Both hands are occluded inside pants pockets; don't add external fingers/thumbs. The far arm is partly occluded by the torso but remains readable along its edge.

Keep the center of mass inside the foot support area. Use slight foot stagger and a soft front knee; avoid a wide fighting squat or a backward lean. Preserve a clear arm/waist contour. Breathing should taper from shoulder motion to almost zero at wrists, because hands stay in pockets.

Use stable `head_outline`, `pocket_anchors`, `foot_anchors` and `torso_motion` guides. If a compatible backend supports line-art/pose control, keep head and lower-leg guides fixed and only nudge torso/shoulder guides. This package was generated with reference-guided edits, not explicit ControlNet or mask controls.
